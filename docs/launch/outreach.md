## (a) Community posts

### What the rules say — and what we couldn't check

Reddit is blocked from this research environment, and web search returned no copy of either
subreddit's current rules. So **nothing below is a quote of their rules.** Read the sidebar,
the rules page and any pinned mod post the day you post; they change.

What to expect, and how we act either way:

- **Reddit-wide:** moderators set their own rules, and subreddit rules are always stricter
  than the site's. Undisclosed promotion is treated as deception. Mods check the poster's
  history, and identical posts across subreddits read as spam.
- **r/ChronicPain:** a large support and venting community for every kind of pain. Expect a
  ban on advertising and self-promotion, and surveys, research and recruitment allowed only
  with mod approval. Many members have been told their pain is "in their head", so a mind-body
  app can land badly here.
- **r/TMSmindbody:** a community built around the mind-body approach (Sarno, PRT, *The Way
  Out*). Expect rules about supportive tone and no medical advice. Promotion of paid programs
  is likely restricted.
- **Facebook PRT and neuroplastic-pain groups:** private groups, with admins approving every
  post. "No promotion" is the usual rule; some allow it once with permission.

**What we do in every community:**

1. Join, read for a week, and comment helpfully before posting (the history matters).
2. **Message the mods first** (modmail, or the Facebook admin) with the draft below, and post
   only with a yes. Offer to post in a weekly thread instead if they have one.
3. First line: "I made this." No link in the post unless the mods say links are fine; offer
   the TestFlight link by DM.
4. One post per community, a few days apart, each written for that community.
5. Answer every reply, the critical ones first. Never argue about whether someone's pain is
   "mind-body".
6. Name the book or Sarno only where the community already does (r/TMSmindbody, PRT groups);
   never in r/ChronicPain.

## (b) Clinician one-pager

For physios, pain psychologists and PRT coaches. One A4 page as a PDF; the same text at
`/clinicians` on the landing page.

> # Attend — somatic tracking practice between sessions
>
> **What it is.** An iPhone app with a 28-day program that follows Pain Reprocessing Therapy's
> order: Understand, Notice, Soften, Live. Each day is a two-minute lesson and a 3–10 minute
> voice-guided somatic tracking practice, done with the eyes closed. There are also one-tap
> practices for a flare, sleep, a body scan and worry, and nature sounds.
>
> **How a patient uses it between sessions.**
> - Starts Day 1 in about a minute: no account, no forms.
> - One lesson and one practice a day, at a time they choose, with one daily reminder (weekly
>   after four quiet days).
> - In a flare or a bad night, one tap starts a short practice.
> - If they choose to, they keep a 0–10 pain check-in, separate from practice.
> - They can bring the Progress screen to your session: practice days, minutes and lessons, with
>   their pain record beside it. Nothing is sent to you or to us; they show you their phone.
>
> **What it doesn't do.**
> - It doesn't diagnose, assess or triage, and it never tells a patient their pain is benign or
>   "safe". Lessons explain the alarm model and invite them to weigh their own signs with you.
> - It isn't therapy and doesn't replace you. It's practice, the way exercises between physio
>   sessions are practice.
> - No scores, no outcome measures, no pain grading, no before-and-after.
> - No account, no data sharing, no analytics. Sessions and check-ins stay on the phone.
> - It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the Pain Reprocessing Therapy Center.
>
> **Safety.** The welcome tells every user that the app can't tell whether a symptom is serious,
> and to contact a professional for anything new, unexplained, worsening quickly or urgent.
> Close attention to the body doesn't suit everyone (trauma, health anxiety); stopping is
> framed as part of the practice.
>
> **Patient codes.** Week 1 is free for everyone. For your patients we'll set up a clinic code
> that gives the full program free for [3 months]. Patients enter it in the App Store or in
> Attend → Settings → Redeem a code. Each clinic gets its own code: we see how many times it's
> been redeemed, never who redeemed it. Email [itai@domain] with your name, clinic and roughly
> how many patients.
>
> **Who made it.** Itai Agami, independent developer. [One true line of background.]
> [itai@domain] · [domain]
