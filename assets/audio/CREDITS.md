# Sound credits

The background recordings under each session are cut from field recordings in the
[radio aporee ::: maps](https://aporee.org/maps/) archive on the Internet Archive, released
under the [Creative Commons Public Domain Mark 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/).
No attribution is required; we credit them with thanks. `tools/make-ambience.py` rebuilds the
loops from these sources.

| File | Recording | Archive item |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ambience-notice.wav` | Mountain stream, Pitões das Júnias, Portugal | [aporee_69234_80475](https://archive.org/details/aporee_69234_80475) |
| `ambience-flare.wav` | Ocean waves, Cox's Bazar beach, Bangladesh | [aporee_55131_63018](https://archive.org/details/aporee_55131_63018) |
| `ambience-sleep.wav` | Rain through the night, Serrinha do Alambari, Brazil | [aporee_54335_62155](https://archive.org/details/aporee_54335_62155) |
| `ambience-fear.wav` | Wind in a pine forest, Miren, Slovenia | [aporee_51715_59066](https://archive.org/details/aporee_51715_59066) |

`turn-cue.wav` and `gong.wav` (synthesised by `tools/make-gong.py`) are the app's own.
